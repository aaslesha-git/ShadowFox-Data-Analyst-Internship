# Task 1 – Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

This project was completed as part of the Beginner Level Data Analyst Internship task at ShadowFox.

The project analyzes a retail sales dataset using Google Sheets and presents the results through a spreadsheet-based dashboard.

## Objectives

* Clean and organize the retail sales dataset.
* Perform basic sales analysis.
* Calculate key business metrics.
* Analyze monthly, category-wise, regional, and product-level performance.
* Create charts and a dashboard.
* Identify useful business insights and recommendations.

## Tools Used

* Google Sheets
* Pivot Tables
* Spreadsheet Formulas
* Charts and Data Visualization
* Dashboard

## Data Cleaning

The dataset was cleaned by:

* Removing duplicate records.
* Handling missing values.
* Standardizing date information.
* Creating a Month field for monthly analysis.

Missing region information was represented as `Unknown` rather than removing those records.

## Analysis Performed

The project includes:

* Monthly Sales Analysis
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Region
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Monthly Profit Analysis
* Key Performance Indicators

## Dashboard

The dashboard contains five visualizations:

1. Monthly Sales Trend
2. Sales by Category
3. Sales by Region
4. Top 10 Products by Sales
5. Monthly Profit Trend

It also includes:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity
* Average Order Value

## Key Findings

* Accessories was the highest-selling category.
* South was the highest-performing region.
* April 2025 recorded the highest monthly sales.
* Monitor was the top-selling product.
* East was the lowest-performing identified region.

## Business Recommendations

The analysis suggests maintaining sufficient stock for high-performing products, studying the reasons behind strong performance in the South region, investigating the April sales peak, and identifying opportunities to improve performance in the East region.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the basic data analyst workflow of data cleaning, spreadsheet analysis, visualization, dashboard creation, and business interpretation.
